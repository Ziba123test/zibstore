ZibStore v117 — Devout Edition + implicit Collection matching

Changed:
- api/server.js
- index.html

Backend:
- Devout Edition is recognized as a paid full-game edition and resolves through Steam Package/SubID, not an Upgrade DLC AppID.
- A seller title may safely match a Steam package whose only extra trailing word is "Collection".
- Added a conservative franchise-prefix anchor (text before ':') for package discovery.
- Admin search shows implicit Collection package candidates when ordinary AppID matching has no strong result.
- Automatic matching prefers no Steam price over binding a paid edition/collection to the wrong AppID.

Frontend:
- Devout Edition counts as "Расширенное" without adding a new badge or dedicated dropdown item.
- A backend packageKind=collection also counts as expanded even if seller omitted the word Collection.
